  Rule R10: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    Orbital Sciences Corporation → ORBCOMM Inc: Pay power penalty if payload exceeds weight limits
    If ORBCOMM agrees to accept Additional Payloads that exceed the maximum operating power and/or weight limits set forth in the Specifications, then Orbital shall pay ORBCOMM as compensation therefor, $10,000 per Kg in excess of 20.31 Kg (inclusive of 1kg grace) for the total Additional Payload mass as set forth in Section 4.5.4 of the Specifications and $10,000 per 5 Watts in excess of 120 Watts (inclusive of 5 Watts margin) of total Additional Payload power as set forth in Section 4.5.5 of the Specifications, in each case per Additional Payload, and in each case pro rated for partial amounts in excess of such maximum weight and/or power. The maximum payment that shall be paid for exceeding the power and/or weight limits shall not exceed $100,000.
    Trigger: When threshold crossed — Additional Payload mass exceeds 20.31 Kg (inclusive of 1kg grace)
      Threshold: payload_mass_kg gt 20.31
    Trigger: When threshold crossed — Additional Payload power exceeds 120 Watts (inclusive of 5 Watts margin)
      Threshold: payload_power_watts gt 120
    Trigger: When condition precedent holds — ORBCOMM agrees to accept Additional Payloads exceeding maximum limits
    Effect: Payment — Weight penalty payment
      Formula: max(0, payload mass kg - 20.31) * 10000
      From: Orbital Sciences Corporation → To: ORBCOMM Inc
      Cap: $100,000.00
    Effect: Payment — Power penalty payment
      Formula: max(0, (payload power watts - 120) / 5) * 10000
      From: Orbital Sciences Corporation → To: ORBCOMM Inc
      Cap: $100,000.00
    Constraint: Maximum cap
      Value: $100,000.00
      Maximum payment cap of $100,000 for exceeding power and/or weight limits (combined)
    Constraint: Pro rata allocation
      Pro-rated for partial amounts in excess of maximum weight and/or power

STATE TRANSITIONS
────────────────────────────────────────
  When in state "Contract effective, payloads in development and manufacturing phase":

    On event "bond deadline approaching":
      Launch date minus 30 days reached, ORBCOMM must provide bond or LC
      Transitions to "Launch approaching, awaiting performance bond or letter of credit from ORBCOMM".

    On event "delivery window reached":
      14-month delivery window reached, grace periods now active
      Transitions to "Within grace period for payload/antenna delivery, no penalties yet".

  When in state "Payloads delivered, pending ORBCOMM acceptance and final payment":

    On event "acceptance with penalty":
      Guard: payload weight kg  >  20.31 or payload power watts  >  120
      ORBCOMM accepts payloads exceeding power/weight limits, Orbital pays penalties
      → Payment: ORBCOMM pays contract price minus holdback [contract price - holdback amount]
      → Payment: Weight penalty for exceeding 20.31 kg at $10,000/kg [max(0, payload weight kg - 20.31) * weight penalty per kg]
      → Payment: Power penalty for exceeding 120W at $10,000 per 5W increment [max(0, (payload power watts - 120) / 5) * power penalty per 5w]
      Transitions to "Payloads accepted and launched, awaiting successful IOT completion".

    On event "rejection":
      ORBCOMM refuses to accept payloads, contract terminates
      → Forfeiture: ORBCOMM rejects payloads as non-conforming beyond acceptable limits
      Transitions to "Contract terminated or obligations ended without full completion".

    On event "acceptance no penalty":
      Guard: payload weight kg  ≤  20.31 and payload power watts  ≤  120
      ORBCOMM accepts payloads meeting specs, pays contract price less holdback
      → Payment: ORBCOMM pays contract price minus 4% holdback ($16,320,000) [contract price - holdback amount]
      Transitions to "Payloads accepted and launched, awaiting successful IOT completion".

  When in state "Launch approaching, awaiting performance bond or letter of credit from ORBCOMM":

    On event "bond provided":
      ORBCOMM provides bond or LC, contract proceeds normally
      → Asset transfer: ORBCOMM delivers performance bond or letter of credit
      Transitions to "Within grace period for payload/antenna delivery, no penalties yet".

    On event "bond not provided":
      ORBCOMM fails to provide bond/LC, must pay holdback immediately
      → Payment: ORBCOMM pays holdback amount upfront due to missing bond/LC [holdback amount]
      Transitions to "Within grace period for payload/antenna delivery, no penalties yet".

  When in state "Payloads accepted and launched, awaiting successful IOT completion":

    On event "iot completed":
      In-orbit testing successfully completed, one-year monitoring period begins
      Transitions to "IOT complete, one-year monitoring period before holdback release".

  When in state "Within grace period for payload/antenna delivery, no penalties yet":

    On event "on time delivery":
      Guard: days late  =  0
      Orbital delivers all payloads within grace period, earns on-time incentive
      → Asset transfer: All payloads and antenna subsystems delivered on time
      → Payment: On-time delivery incentive of $204,000 [on time incentive]
      Transitions to "Payloads delivered, pending ORBCOMM acceptance and final payment".

    On event "early delivery":
      Guard: days early  >  0
      Orbital delivers early, earns both on-time and early delivery incentives
      → Asset transfer: All payloads and antenna subsystems delivered early
      → Payment: On-time delivery incentive [on time incentive]
      → Payment: Early delivery incentive at $1,700/day, capped at $100,000 [days early * early delivery per day]
      Transitions to "Payloads delivered, pending ORBCOMM acceptance and final payment".

    On event "grace period expired":
      Grace period exhausted without delivery, late penalties begin accruing
      Transitions to "Grace period exhausted, late delivery penalties accruing daily".

  When in state "Grace period exhausted, late delivery penalties accruing daily":

    On event "termination for default":
      Contract terminated due to prolonged late delivery or other material breach
      → Forfeiture: Contract terminated for excessive delay or other default
      Transitions to "Contract terminated or obligations ended without full completion".

    On event "late delivery":
      Guard: days late  >  0
      Orbital delivers late, pays liquidated damages
      → Asset transfer: Payloads and antenna subsystems delivered late
      → Payment: Liquidated damages at $8,500/day (max $25k/day), capped at $2,550,000 [days late * late delivery per day]
      Transitions to "Payloads delivered, pending ORBCOMM acceptance and final payment".

  When in state "IOT complete, one-year monitoring period before holdback release":

    On event "one year success":
      Guard: payloads operating  =  total payloads
      All payloads successfully operating one year after IOT, holdback fully released
      → Payment: Full holdback released, all payloads operating successfully for one year [holdback amount]
      Transitions to "All obligations met, holdback released, contract successfully completed".

    On event "one year partial failure":
      Guard: payloads operating  <  total payloads
      One or more payloads failed during one-year period, partial holdback release
      → Payment: Pro-rata holdback payment based on successfully operating payloads [holdback amount * (payloads operating / total payloads)]
      → Forfeiture: Orbital forfeits portion of holdback for non-operating payloads
      Transitions to "All obligations met, holdback released, contract successfully completed".
