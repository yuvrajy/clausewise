RULES AND OBLIGATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────
  Rule R1: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    Orbital → ORBCOMM: Design, manufacture, test and deliver EDU Unit and Additional Payloads
    Orbital shall design, manufacture, test and deliver one (1) payload (without antenna) engineering development unit (the 'EDU Unit') and six (6) additional Automatic Identification System (AIS) Signal payloads (the 'Additional Payloads') in accordance with the Statement of Work and Specifications attached hereto, including (without limitation) the delivery schedule set forth in Section 2.1.1 of the SOW (the 'Delivery Schedule').
    Trigger: On date — Effective Date established (both parties signed AND first Milestone Payment received)
    Effect: Asset transfer — Delivery of EDU Unit and six Additional Payloads per Delivery Schedule
    Constraint: Grace period
      Value: $60.00
      Duration: 60 days
      Grace period for Additional Payloads (less antenna subsystem) - no penalty during this period
    Constraint: Grace period
      Value: $90.00
      Duration: 90 days
      Grace period for antenna subsystem - no penalty during this period
    Exception: R10

  Rule R13: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    ORBCOMM → Orbital: Deliver performance bond or letter of credit
    In order to holdback such amounts, ORBCOMM shall, not later than thirty (30) days prior to launch, deliver to ORBITAL a performance bond or letter of credit to receive such payment, failing which ORBCOMM shall make the Holdback Payment to Orbital or a mutually agreeable third party escrow agent at that time.
    Trigger: After time period — 30 days prior to launch
      Duration: -30 days
    Effect: Asset transfer — Delivery of performance bond or letter of credit
      From: ORBCOMM → To: Orbital
    Exception: R14

  Rule R14: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    ORBCOMM → Orbital: Make Holdback Payment if bond/LOC not delivered
    If ORBCOMM fails to deliver bond/letter of credit, ORBCOMM shall make the Holdback Payment to Orbital or a mutually agreeable third party escrow agent at that time, but subject to refund by Orbital (or release to ORBCOMM by such escrow agent), on a pro rata basis, if one or more of the Additional Payloads are not so successfully operating on said first anniversary date.
    Trigger: Upon absence of event — ORBCOMM fails to deliver performance bond or letter of credit by 30 days prior to launch
      Duration: -30 days
    Effect: Payment — Holdback Payment to Orbital or escrow agent
      Formula: 680000
      From: ORBCOMM → To: Orbital
    Constraint: Pro rata allocation
      Subject to pro rata refund if one or more Additional Payloads not successfully operating on first anniversary date

  Rule R16: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    Orbital → ORBCOMM: Deliver Optional Payload if option exercised
    Delivery of each Optional Payload (less the antenna subsystem) will be fourteen (14) months (plus sixty (60) days grace period) and fourteen (14) months (plus ninety (90) days grace period) for the antenna subsystem associated therewith, after ORBCOMM's written direction is received exercising this option and the first milestone payment for each Optional Payload in the amount of $200,000 is received.
    Trigger: When condition precedent holds — ORBCOMM exercises option in writing AND first milestone payment of $200,000 received
      Duration: 14 months
    Effect: Asset transfer — Delivery of Optional Payload
    Constraint: Grace period
      Value: $60.00
      Duration: 60 days
      Grace period for Optional Payload (less antenna subsystem)
    Constraint: Grace period
      Value: $90.00
      Duration: 90 days
      Grace period for Optional Payload antenna subsystem

  Rule R17: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    ORBCOMM → Orbital: Pay for Optional Payloads
    The payment schedule for the Optional Payloads shall correspond as closely as possible (in terms of time and percentage of total price) to the AP Milestones set forth on Exhibit E-1.
    Trigger: When condition precedent holds — Optional Payload milestones completed
    Effect: Payment — Payment of Optional Payload milestone amounts
      Formula: 2200000 * (milestone percentage / 100)
      From: ORBCOMM → To: Orbital

  Rule R18: Obligation (inactive at formation)

    ORBCOMM → Orbital: Pay for additional environmental testing if directed

    Trigger: Upon event — ORBCOMM directs additional environmental testing in writing
    Effect: Payment — Payment for additional environmental testing
      Formula: 94000 if first payload else 26000
      From: ORBCOMM → To: Orbital

  Rule R2: Obligation (inactive at formation)

    Effect: Asset transfer — Provision of deliverables, documentation, and services

  Rule R20: Obligation (inactive at formation)

  Rule R25: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    Orbital → ORBCOMM: Repair and correct defects revealed in integration testing

    Effect: Asset transfer — Repair and correction of Additional Payloads

  Rule R4: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    Orbital → ORBCOMM: Pay liquidated delay damages for late delivery
    If one or more of the Additional Payloads is not delivered in accordance with the requirements of this Amendment within fourteen (14) months of the Effective Date plus sixty (60) days grace period (no penalty shall be incurred in the grace period) for the Additional Payloads (less the antenna subsystem) and fourteen (14) months plus ninety (90) days grace period for the antenna subsystem associated therewith, starting on the first day after such applicable grace period has been exhausted, Orbital shall pay ORBCOMM liquidated delay damages of 0.05% ($8,500) per day for each Additional Payload that has not been so delivered for each day of delay.
    Trigger: After time period — Delivery not made within 14 months plus applicable grace period after Effective Date
      Duration: 14 months plus 60 days for payloads, 14 months plus 90 days for antenna
    Effect: Payment — Liquidated delay damages payment
      Formula: days late * 8500
      From: Orbital → To: ORBCOMM
      Cap: $2,550,000.00
    Constraint: Maximum cap
      Value: $2,550,000.00
      Total late delivery damages capped at 15% of Price ($2,550,000)
    Constraint: Maximum cap
      Value: $25,000.00
      Daily late delivery damages capped at $25,000 per day
    Constraint: Controlling provision
      Late charges determined by greatest lateness of any single item associated with same Additional Payload, not additive for separate items
    Constraint: Aggregate constraint
      Late delivery damages inclusive of power and weight penalties described in clause 3.(iv)
    Exception: R10

  Rule R40: Prohibition (inactive at formation)
    Orbital → ORBCOMM: Redeploy work items to other programs without consent
    Supplies, prints, materials, components, subsystems, and systems as associated with such work shall not be redeployed to other programs without ORBCOMM's consent.

  Rule R5: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    ORBCOMM → Orbital: Pay on-time delivery incentive
    If the EDU Unit and all of the Additional Payloads are delivered in accordance with the requirements of this Amendment by the date specified in the Delivery Schedule, ORBCOMM shall pay Orbital a single on-time delivery incentive of 1.2% of the Price ($204,000).
    Trigger: When condition precedent holds — EDU Unit and all Additional Payloads delivered by Delivery Schedule date
    Effect: Payment — Single on-time delivery incentive payment
      Formula: 204000
      From: ORBCOMM → To: Orbital

  Rule R6: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    ORBCOMM → Orbital: Pay early delivery incentive
    If all of the Additional Payloads are so delivered on time, for each day that the Additional Payloads are so delivered early, ORBCOMM shall pay Orbital 0.01% of Price ($1700) per day of early delivery.
    Trigger: When condition precedent holds — All Additional Payloads delivered early (before Delivery Schedule date)
    Effect: Payment — Early delivery incentive payment
      Formula: days early * 1700
      From: ORBCOMM → To: Orbital
      Cap: $100,000.00
    Constraint: Maximum cap
      Value: $100,000.00
      Total early delivery incentive capped at $100,000 for all Additional Payloads combined
    Constraint: Controlling provision
      For early delivery calculation, least early item associated with particular Additional Payload controls

  Rule R8: Obligation (inactive at formation)
    Orbital → ORBCOMM: Pay power and weight penalties
    If ORBCOMM agrees to accept Additional Payloads that exceed the maximum operating power and/or weight limits set forth in the Specifications, then Orbital shall pay ORBCOMM as compensation therefor, $10,000 per Kg in excess of 20.31 Kg (inclusive of 1kg grace) for the total Additional Payload mass as set forth in Section 4.5.4 of the Specifications and $10,000 per 5 Watts in excess of 120 Watts (inclusive of 5 Watts margin) of total Additional Payload power as set forth in Section 4.5.5 of the Specifications, in each case per Additional Payload, and in each case pro rated for partial amounts in excess of such maximum weight and/or power.
    Trigger: When threshold crossed — Additional Payload exceeds weight limit
      Threshold: payload_weight_kg gt 20.31
    Trigger: When threshold crossed — Additional Payload exceeds power limit
      Threshold: payload_power_watts gt 120
    Effect: Payment — Weight penalty payment
      Formula: (payload weight kg - 20.31) * 10000
      From: Orbital → To: ORBCOMM
    Effect: Payment — Power penalty payment
      Formula: ((payload power watts - 120) / 5) * 10000
      From: Orbital → To: ORBCOMM
    Constraint: Maximum cap
      Value: $100,000.00
      Combined power and/or weight penalties capped at $100,000
    Constraint: Pro rata allocation
      Penalties pro-rated for partial amounts in excess of limits
